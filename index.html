About Me

My dream job is not that unusual, I want to be a user interface designer. They help design the vibe of the website, and make sites easy to use. I plan to achieve this by finding an internship that would give me experience related to designing websites. I know how it feels to use a website and be disappointed by its interface, whether I am playing a game and can’t pause it without triggering an ad, or accidentally deleting an important document rather than copying it. These are flaws in the user interface I believe I can help fix as a user interface designer, which is also my primary reason for choosing this as my dream career.

Next, my background with language and technology has influenced how I think and solve problems. Speaking multiple languages has helped me understand that people process information in different ways, which affects how I focus on organization. Through coding classes and my involvement in the Computer Science Honor Society, I have learned to work through problems carefully and improve my ideas until they make sense to others.

My extracurricular activities have also shaped the way I approach my work. Playing in the junior varsity girls soccer team and badminton has taught me how to stay focused, work with a team, and make quick adjustments when needed. Baking has shown me the importance of following steps, paying attention to details, and practicing to get better. These experiences have helped me develop patience and consistency in the way I complete projects.

In conclusion, these experiences show the mindset I bring to Ul and UX design, one rooted in empathy, adaptability, and attention to detail. Thank you for taking the time to learn more about me, and I invite you to explore the rest of my website to see how these qualities are reflected in my work.
